How to play

  • Every number is a single digit from 1 to 9.
  • Some are bold. Some are tilted and faded. Some take the colour of whatever they sit on.
  • Tap to find: tap a number to ring it. Wrong taps count as misses.
  • Count only: no tapping. Count them by eye, enter your total, then check.
  • Each puzzle number gives the same picture every time, so you can share it.
